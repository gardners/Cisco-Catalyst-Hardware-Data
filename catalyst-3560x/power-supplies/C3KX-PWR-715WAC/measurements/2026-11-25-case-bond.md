# M1 — Owner-reported case and power-contact continuity

Reported **2026-11-25** during the PSU output-reference investigation. The owner subsequently clarified that testing used the multimeter's **continuity beeper mode**, not resistance mode. This records the observations separately from the external conversion reports in [pinout-research.md](../pinout-research.md).

## Observations

| Path | Owner's reported result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Mains protective-earth connection to metal PSU case | Bond reported; initially described as 0 Ω | Continuity-mode test; numeric resistance not established |
| Metal case to each of four visible blade pins | No continuity beep on any of the four | Meter's beep threshold unspecified; resistance and `OL` not measured |

Only **one** of the two PSU specimens was tested; the owner plans to check both. The tested OEM/specimen and complete label have not yet been associated with this report. The preceding proposed test was unplugged, discharged and disconnected from the switch, but those conditions were not explicitly restated with the result; confirmation is pending. The instrument, polarity and exact contact positions are also unrecorded. Do not apply this result to both OEM specimens.

The owner describes **four visible blade pins**. Their electrical grouping into the archive's left/right power-contact assemblies has not been measured in this report. No pairwise continuity or polarity is inferred from that count.

## Interpretation

The reported case-to-earth continuity is consistent with a protective-earth bond. The absence of a case-to-blade beep is consistent with a floating DC output, but establishes only that the meter's continuity detector did not trigger. **It does not establish high resistance, an open circuit or the absence of a conductive path.** No resistance-mode values or `OL` readings were obtained.

If the settled blade-to-case readings are high resistance or `OL` with the isolated module unpowered, they would support the narrower conclusion **no direct DC output-to-case bond detected in that state**. That would still not establish the permitted output-to-earth voltage, powered behaviour, mains insulation rating, control/standby reference or connections introduced by the original switch.

For the general distinction between floating and earthed outputs, see [Matsusada Precision's manufacturer explanation](https://www.matsusada.com/support/terms/ps/floating.html). It is background terminology, not a specification for these Cisco modules.

## Next information to retain

- Full module identity for each specimen, whether unplugged and outside the switch, and other attached connections; repeat on both OEM variants.
- Settled resistance for each visible blade pin to case, including `OL`, range and any changing reading; repeat with reversed meter polarity if needed.
- Physical registration and pairwise blade continuity to establish the number of distinct power nets.
- Signal-return relationships, especially S10/S12 to the blades and case, before connecting control or standby circuitry to a new system ground.

Keep the case's protective-earth connection intact. Follow the [probe plan](../probe-plan.md) for subsequent measurements. A DMM resistance check does not establish an insulation-withstand rating or the suitability of a new output-to-chassis bond.
