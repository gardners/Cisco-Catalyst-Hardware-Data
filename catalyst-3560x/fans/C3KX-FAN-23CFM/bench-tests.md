# Fan cartridge investigation worksheet

The following are **unperformed tests**, not claims about observed behaviour.

1. Photograph both connector faces, mating shell and contact markings against a scale.
2. Record contact number, lead colour, continuity to motor terminals, chassis and other signals with cartridge unpowered.
3. Obtain the specific Delta model's electrical and speed-control documentation, not merely a generic 40 mm fan pinout.
4. Identify the original Cisco motherboard's fan power/control/reference signals if available.
5. Only with a verified fused and current-limited isolated test fixture, characterise tach and speed-control at 12 V; do not apply power to unidentified pins.
6. Record the differences (if any) between chassis cartridges and PSU internal fans.
